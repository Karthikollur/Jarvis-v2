# Board game club handbook

River Lantern Board-Game Club meets every Sunday at four. The guest fee is 3 dollars, collected in the jar by the door. The library of games stays in the blue cupboard, and a member signs the card before taking a box home.

The chair sets out tables before anyone arrives. A show of hands settles which box is played first. Tea is poured at the break. The paper list of boxes stays on the inside of the cupboard door, and a box that comes back late is noted on that list before it is shelved. The jar is counted at the end of the evening and the coins are written in the small book that lives behind the list.

Tables are folded and stood against the far wall once the last box is back in the cupboard. The door card is wiped and returned to its hook. A member who wants a box for the week writes a name and a date on the card, and the chair checks that the pieces are complete when the box comes home. Tea cups are rinsed in the side room and left to dry on the rack, not stacked wet inside the cupboard.
