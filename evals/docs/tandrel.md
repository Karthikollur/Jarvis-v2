# Hill trip plan

The hill trip starts the trailhead walk on 6 September. Two nights are at the cabin Ostermere. The spare key is in the tin box on the rear step. Pack a wool layer and the paper map. The path leaves the lane at the green gate and climbs to the cabin. Return is on the morning after the second night.

If rain starts, the wool layer goes on at the green gate before the climb. Supper is cooked at the cabin on both evenings. The booking paper stays in the bag, and the map stays in the front pocket where it can be checked without a full stop. The tin box is closed again once the key is out, so the step stays clear for the walk back.

Water is carried from the tap at the trailhead, because the cabin tap is slow. The stove is lit only after the windows are cracked, and the wood stack is under the eaves on the north side. Boots stay on the mat inside the door. On the second morning the key goes back in the tin box, the latch is dropped, and the green gate is shut from the lane side before the walk down to the road.
