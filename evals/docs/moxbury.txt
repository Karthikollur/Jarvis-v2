Scooter brake note. The little blue scooter had its brake pads replaced on 19 April. The pads are Lorbeck, size 9 by 18, one pair on the front wheel. The front cable was adjusted the same day so the lever meets the grip after a short pull. The wheel was spun and the pads were not rubbing. A rag wiped the hub. The old pads went into the metal bin in the service bay. The rear brake was left as it was because the lever still felt firm.

The axle nut was tightened to the figure printed on the swingarm, then marked with a dab of paint so a later glance shows if it has moved. The cable housing was checked for cracks where it meets the lever, and the adjuster was left with a little thread still showing. After the spin, the scooter was rolled a few metres in the bay so the lever could be felt under a real stop. The rag was hung on the hook beside the bin, not left on the tyre.

The paper job card names the front wheel only. The rear lever was squeezed and released three times, the feel stayed the same, and no parts came off that end. Dust from the old pads was brushed off the hub before the new pair went on. The blue scooter was put back against the wall with the stand down, and the job card was clipped to the bay board before the bay light was switched off.
